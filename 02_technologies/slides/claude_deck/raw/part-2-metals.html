AM process: metals

Metal AM melts or bonds powder and wire: in a bed, through a nozzle, or by impact.

  • Powder bed fusion (LPBF)
  • Directed energy deposition: powder
  • Directed energy deposition: wire
  • Cold spray: bonding without melting
  • Parameters, makers, references

EXED Polytechnique - Additive Manufacturing - 02 Technologies by Andrei Constantinescu 2027

Metals start from powder or wire. Three of the four routes melt the metal (laser, electron beam or arc: FR fusion, DE Schmelzen); cold spray bonds solid particles by plastic deformation at impact (FR projection à froid, DE Kaltgasspritzen). ISO/ASTM 52900: PBF-LB/M, DED-LB/M powder, DED-Arc/M wire; cold spray is classed under DED or as solid-state AM.
