Space: NASA Commercial Crew requires loss of crew below 1/500 for ascent and 1/500 for entry, 1/270 for the whole mission. NASA NPR 8705.4A defines payload risk classes A (very low risk, e.g. JWST, Hubble) to D (high risk tolerance, e.g. CYGNSS, CubeSats); these classes grade mission importance, not danger to people.
