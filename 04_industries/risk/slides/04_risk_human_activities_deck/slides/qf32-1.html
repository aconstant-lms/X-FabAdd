ATSB AO-2010-089 (final, 27 June 2013): 4 November 2010, VH-OQA, climbing through 7,000 ft (2 100 m) after departing Singapore Changi; 5 flight crew, 24 cabin crew, 440 passengers; uncontained failure of the IP turbine disc of the No. 2 engine overhead Batam Island. A section of about 43 % of the disc, about 70 kg, was recovered from a property on Batam Island; two radial fractures and a circumferential fracture in the drive arm, all overstress, no pre-existing material defect. It took about 50 minutes for the crew to complete the initial ECAM procedures. Third-party photos in the report (passengers, Qantas crew, AAIB Singapore, Posmetro, Rolls-Royce, Airbus) are not used.
