Rule: a part may only be counted on to be inspected if the defect that kills it can be seen by the method used, where it starts.

Sioux City: the crack started from a cavity at a nitrogen-rich hard alpha inclusion at the bore; FPI is a surface method read by eye; NTSB cites human factors limitations; afterwards ultrasonic (AD 89-20-01) and eddy current (AD 91-12-09) inspection, triple vacuum melting, AC 33.14-1. Eschede: highest stress and crack origin on the inner face of the tyre, hidden in the assembled wheel. Adamuz: provisional CIAF hypothesis that the rail broke at a weld before the Iryo train passed; notches on wheels of three earlier trains. Qantas 32: the wall thickness of the oil feed stub pipe could not be seen by the inspector after assembly; a CMM report error statement was not acted on. Columbia: no means to inspect the wing leading edge in orbit; after 2003 the Orbiter Boom Sensor System and imagery inspections were introduced.
