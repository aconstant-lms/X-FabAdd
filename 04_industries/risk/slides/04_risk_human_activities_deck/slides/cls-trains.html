Steel is not in the SIL table

  • SILs apply to signalling and control electronics
  • Wheels (EN 13979-1), axles (EN 13103/13104) and rails: designed for fatigue, kept safe by inspection
  • One wheel, one rail weld: a single load path, a Class A part
  • Eschede and Adamuz, in Part 3, are both mechanical

IEC 61508; EN 50126, EN 50128, EN 50129; ERA Safety Overview 2021

SIL / THR bands follow IEC 61508 (continuous mode, probability of dangerous failure per hour), used by EN 50126 (RAMS), EN 50128 (software) and EN 50129 (hardware, safety case). Example allocations vary between projects and published tables disagree; keep SIL 4 for interlocking and ATP/ETCS, the rest is indicative. ERA Safety Overview 2021: about 0.033 passenger fatalities per billion passenger-km in the EU.
