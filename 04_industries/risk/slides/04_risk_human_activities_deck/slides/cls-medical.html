EU MDR 2017/745 Annex VIII Rule 8: implantable devices are class IIb unless they are total or partial joint replacements (class III, except ancillary screws, wedges, plates and instruments) or in contact with the spinal column; devices in contact with the heart or central circulatory system are class III. In the US many 3D-printed joint implants are FDA class II (510(k)) while coronary stents are class III (PMA). ABSORB III: device thrombosis 1.5 % (Absorb) vs 0.7 % (Xience) at 12 months; Abbott withdrew Absorb worldwide in September 2017 after more than 150,000 implantations, following an FDA safety alert in March 2017. DePuy ASR metal-on-metal hip recall (2010): another late failure found by registries.
