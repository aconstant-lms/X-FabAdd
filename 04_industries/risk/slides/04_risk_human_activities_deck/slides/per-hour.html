Colours and order follow the categories of the previous slide. Hatched bars are device failure events for implants, not deaths. Bars show the spread between sources and countries. Aviation per hour hides that most of the risk is concentrated in take-off and landing. Skydiving assumes about 0.5 h of exposure per jump; Himalayan climbs about 1000 h of expedition; Shuttle about 250 h per mission; crew ascent 1/500 over 10-15 minutes.
