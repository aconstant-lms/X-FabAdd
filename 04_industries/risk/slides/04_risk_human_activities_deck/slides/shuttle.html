Challenger, 28 January 1986

  • Booster field-joint O-rings: Criticality 1 since 1982, no backup — a Class A item
  • Erosion seen on earlier flights; the launch constraint was waived again and again
  • Cold launch decided against the engineers' advice: 7 crew lost

Columbia, 1 February 2003

  • Foam insulation, not a structural item, shed at nearly every launch: treated as 'in family'
  • A foam block breaches the wing's carbon leading edge, a Class A part
  • Hot plasma enters the wing at re-entry: 7 crew lost

Lesson: both chains passed through management decisions on known anomalies. A NASA review later put the first Shuttle flights at about 1 in 10.
