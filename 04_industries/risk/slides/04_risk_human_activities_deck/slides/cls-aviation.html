AVIATION · EASA CM-S-008 · CS 25.1309

Four classes of parts, set by the effect of their failure

ClassEffect of the part's failureConditionAllowed per flight hourTypical parts
A · HighDirectly affects continued safe flight and landing; serious or fatal injuriesCatastrophic / Hazardous< 10⁻⁹ / < 10⁻⁷Engine discs, fan blades, wing spars, pressure bulkhead, flight-control actuators
B · MediumIndirectly affects safe flight; minor injuries, large rise in crew workloadMajor< 10⁻⁵Secondary structure, fuel and hydraulic lines, brakes
C · LowNo effect on safe flight; slight loss of function or comfortMinor< 10⁻³Cabin interior: bin latches, seat parts, ducts
D · NegligibleNo safety effectNo safety effectNo requirementTrim, covers, placard holders

Two rules for Class A: below 10⁻⁹ per flight hour, and no single failure may lead to a catastrophe, however unlikely.

Targets apply to a failure condition of the system, not to one part. Structure relies on safety factors, A/B-basis allowables and damage tolerance.
