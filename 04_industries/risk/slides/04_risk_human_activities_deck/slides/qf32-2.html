ATSB AO-2010-089: nominal wall thickness of the oil feed stub pipe in the counter bore 0.91 mm; minimum permissible with worst-case tolerances 0.82 mm; the fractured pipe varied from 1.42 mm down to 0.35 mm; the fatigue crack coincided with the thinnest wall section. Figure 29 shows the bearing chamber service pipes (oil feed, scavenge, vent, IP8 air) on the HP/IP support structure; the oil feed stub pipe is the short pipe welded in the hub at the inner end of the circled oil feed line. The misalignment came from the machining datum and fixture; the wall thickness could not be seen by the inspector and a CMM report error statement was not acted on.
