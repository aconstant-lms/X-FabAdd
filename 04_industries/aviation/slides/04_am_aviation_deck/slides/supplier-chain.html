The design-and-build supplier chain: who does what

StepOEM (GA-ASI)Supplier (Divergent)Note
Mission and requirements●OEM defines loads, interfaces, payload
Structural design (AI, topology)○●Supplier designs to the OEM’s requirements
Design review and integration●●Joint: interfaces, weight targets
Printing, inspection, digital twin●Supplier’s machines and process data
Robotic assembly of the structure●Supplier delivers an assembled airframe
Systems, flight test●OEM integrates and flies
Airworthiness evidence●●Shared; DAWGS programme for the process
Variants and rate●●Supplier reprints without new tooling

Key point: the supplier owns design, process and part data, so it can optimise and help qualify; the OEM keeps the aircraft and its certification.

● = leads, ○ = provides input. Our reading of the GA-ASI / Divergent model from public statements: GA-ASI’s AM, aircraft-integrity, materials and design teams work with Divergent to adapt, apply and qualify DAPS for GA-ASI production.
