Airbus A350: from printed brackets to 7-metre titanium parts

[Picture: A350 cargo-door-surround wire-DED part]

airbus.com › newsroom › “titanium 3D printing” (Jan 2026)

  • 2017: first printed titanium bracket on a series Airbus (Arconic, Austin)
  • 2026: plasma wire-DED titanium parts in the A350 cargo-door surround
  • Near-net-shape parts up to 7 m long, then machined

Company

Airbus with Arconic (now Howmet) and Norsk Titanium

Printer / process

Laser powder bed and e-beam (Arconic); plasma wire DED

Gains

Avoids recycling 80–95% of bought titanium; weeks instead of months

Problems / challenges

Ultrasonic inspection and machining still needed; energy sources still evaluated

Source: Howmet/Arconic, Sept 2017; Airbus newsroom and Metal AM, Jan 2026
