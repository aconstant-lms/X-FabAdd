Liebherr-Aerospace: printed spoiler-actuator valve block

[Picture: A380 spoiler valve block]

liebherr.com › press 13 Apr 2017

  • First printed primary-flight-control hydraulic part flown on an Airbus (A380 test flight, 30 March 2017)
  • Same performance as the forged titanium valve block
  • Then serial A350 parts: nose-gear bracket (2019), cargo-door actuator and valve (2022), flex shaft

Company

Liebherr-Aerospace (Lindenberg, DE; HQ Toulouse) with TU Chemnitz

Printer / process

Laser powder bed, titanium powder

Gains

35% lighter, fewer parts, minimal titanium waste

Problems / challenges

Whole chain still to optimise: powder, laser parameters, post-processing

Source: Liebherr-Aerospace, Apr 2017; 3DPrint.com
