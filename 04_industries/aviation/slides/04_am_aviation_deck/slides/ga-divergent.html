GA-ASI and Divergent started a joint development programme in 2022 and announced the partnership in Feb 2024: a small UAS aerostructure, AI-driven and topology-optimised, printed as an integrated metal structure, inspected via a full digital twin and assembled by robots without tooling in under 20 minutes. In June 2025 Divergent delivered the metallic core structure of a hybrid ground- and air-launched small UAS. GA-ASI also printed a five-foot LPBF airframe section with DoD and Divergent.
