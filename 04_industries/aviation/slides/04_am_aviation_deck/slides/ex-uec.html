UEC VK-1600V: up to 70% of castings replaced by printed parts

[Picture: VK-1600V demonstrator / printed first-support housing]

rostec.ru › news › VK-1600V; airframer.com

  • Helicopter engine for the Ka-62, designed entirely in 3D (no paper drawings) at UEC-Klimov
  • Up to 70% of castings printed, incl. hot-section parts and the first-support housing (made with UEC’s Chernyshev plant, Moscow)
  • 1,400 hp take-off, 210 kg dry weight; demonstrator 2021

Company

United Engine Corporation (Rostec): UEC-Klimov, Chernyshev (Moscow)

Printer / process

Rostec Additive Technologies Center: Russia’s largest laser machine, Russian materials

Gains

Lead time from 6 months to 3 weeks; lighter parts

Problems / challenges

Type certificate planned for 2023 and series for 2024: current status unclear

Source: Rostec/UEC, May 2021; Airframer; Rostec ATC, May 2021; www1.ru, Nov 2024

EXED Polytechnique - Additive Manufacturing - 04 Industries by Andrei Constantinescu 2027

The 70% figure is the share of castings replaced in the demonstrator; Rostec also said more than 10% of the engine’s parts would be printed. Treat as a prototype programme; independent data are scarce.
