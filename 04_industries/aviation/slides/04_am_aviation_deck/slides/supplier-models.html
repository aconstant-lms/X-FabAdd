Why build-to-print alone is not attractive under certification

  • In AM the material and process specifications are part of the approved design, like a drawing (FAA)
  • Key process variables are frozen: a new machine, powder or parameter set means requalification (FAA AC 33.15-3, EASA CM-S-008)
  • The part maker is also the material maker: the supplier pays for machine qualification, powder control and testing
  • Without design authority it cannot consolidate parts, so it competes on price with casting and machining
