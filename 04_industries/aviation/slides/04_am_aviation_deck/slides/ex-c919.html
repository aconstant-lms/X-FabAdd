Company

COMAC (Shanghai); suppliers BLT (Xi’an) and FalconTech

Printer / process

Laser powder bed; FalconTech used foreign-brand machines

Gains

Lighter titanium parts, shorter lead times, domestic capability

Problems / challenges

Early reliance on imported machines; mostly secondary structure
