STORY 1 · GE LEAP FUEL NOZZLE

The fuel nozzle that proved metal AM could fly

1

The problem

The LEAP combustor needed a nozzle tip with intricate internal fuel passages. Made conventionally, it was about 20 machined parts, brazed and welded together.

2

The bet

GE combustion engineer Marie McMasters led a confidential project with Morris Technologies, a Cincinnati AM shop. GE bought Morris in 2012 and fixed the parameters for series production.

3

The result

One printed cobalt-chrome part: 25% lighter, 5× more durable, no brazing.
